OpsCore Vendor Mail Sender

An internal operations tool by Innovatiview India Ltd.

About the app

OpsCore Vendor Mail Sender is an internal web application used by Innovatiview India Ltd. employees to prepare and send exam-deployment emails to security and facility vendors. It helps the Operations team send accurate, consistent emails in bulk and keep an audit record of what was sent.

What the app does

  • Lets an authorised employee sign in with their official Innovatiview Google account (@innovatiview.in or @innovatiview.com).
  • Prepares deployment emails for vendors from data the employee uploads or enters.
  • Sends those emails through the signed-in employee's own Gmail account.
  • Records a dispatch and login audit log, and remembers vendor details, in the company's Google Sheet.

Who can use it

The app is only for employees of Innovatiview India Ltd. Accounts from any other domain are denied access.

Google permissions used

  • Email address: to identify the signed-in employee and confirm the company domain.
  • Gmail (send only): to send vendor emails on the employee's behalf. The app cannot read, list or delete any email.
  • Google Sheets: to write the audit log and vendor memory to the company's own spreadsheet.
