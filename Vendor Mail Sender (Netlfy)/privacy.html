Privacy Policy

OpsCore Vendor Mail Sender · Innovatiview India Ltd. · Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how OpsCore Vendor Mail Sender ("the app"), an internal tool of Innovatiview India Ltd. ("we", "us"), handles information when an employee signs in with Google. The app is available only to our employees.

1. Information we access

  • Google account email address, used to verify that the user belongs to an authorised Innovatiview domain.
  • Gmail send permission (gmail.send), used only to send the emails the user chooses to send. The app does not read, search, list, modify or delete any mailbox content.
  • Google Sheets access, used only to read and write our own audit spreadsheet (login audit, dispatch audit and vendor memory).
  • Data entered or uploaded by the user (for example vendor names, contact emails, phone numbers and centre details) in order to prepare vendor emails.

2. How we use the information

  • To authenticate employees and restrict access to company domains.
  • To send vendor deployment emails requested by the user.
  • To keep an internal audit trail and avoid duplicate dispatches.

3. Storage

The app runs in the user's browser and has no database of its own. Session details and small preferences are kept in the browser's local storage on the user's device and are cleared on sign out. Audit records are stored only in the company's own Google Sheet, which is controlled by Innovatiview India Ltd.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising. Emails sent through the app are delivered to the vendor recipients chosen by the user. Data is shared with Google only as needed to operate Google sign-in, Gmail and Google Sheets.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Security

Access is limited to authorised company accounts and communication with Google APIs takes place over HTTPS. Users can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Retention and deletion

Audit records are retained in the company's Google Sheet for as long as needed for operations. To request deletion of your records, contact us at the email below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
